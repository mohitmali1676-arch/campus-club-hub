# campus-club-hub

A centralized web platform for college student clubs to manage event registrations, room approvals, budgets, and member recruitments.

---

### Product Concept: College Club & Event Management Platform

* **Overview:** A unified web and mobile application designed to streamline campus extracurriculars by connecting college administrations, student club heads, and the general student body within a single ecosystem.
* **Core Problem Solved:** Eliminates fragmented communication across unstructured chat groups, tracks club budgets and room bookings reliably, automates event check-ins, and replaces paper-based permission workflows.

---

### Key Features

* **Role-Based Portals:** Dedicated access levels for Administrators (faculty/dean approval), Club Leaders (event creation, budgeting, announcements), and Students (discovery, registration, attendance).
* **Automated Event & Attendance Lifecycle:** Digital ticket generation with dynamic QR codes for rapid, fraud-proof check-ins at campus venues.
* **Administrative Approval Workflow:** Digital submission and real-time tracking for event permits, auditorium bookings, and funding approvals.
* **Centralized Notice & Recruitment Board:** Central hub for club recruitment forms, interview scheduling, updates, and inter-college fest notices.
* **Budget & Expense Ledger:** Transparent tracking of club funds, sponsorship entries, and expense receipt uploads.
